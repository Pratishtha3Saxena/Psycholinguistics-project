Fast package for mid-eval

Included files:
1. psycholinguistics_curated_eval.py
   - Runs the baseline on the frozen first 300 validation items.
   - Optionally exports the frozen subset as CSV.
   - Optionally evaluates a curated perturbation CSV.
   - Keeps baseline and perturbations separate.

2. winogrande_300_metadata_template.csv
   - Label the frozen 300 items.
   - Mark built-in contrast pairs and items safe for perturbation.

3. curated_perturbation_seed_sheet.csv
   - Small investigative starter set with 9 manually written perturbations:
     * 3 semantic reasoning
     * 3 lexical/surface
     * 3 structural/discourse
   - Validate each row before final analysis.

Recommended immediate workflow:
A. Run baseline:
   python3 psycholinguistics_curated_eval.py --model bert-base-uncased --split validation --max-items 300 --output-dir outputs_curated --export-dataset-sheet

B. Open curated_perturbation_seed_sheet.csv and mark:
   grammar_valid, meaning_preserved, gold_preserved, keep_for_analysis, validator_notes

C. Re-run with curated perturbations:
   python3 psycholinguistics_curated_eval.py --model bert-base-uncased --split validation --max-items 300 --output-dir outputs_curated --curated-perturbations curated_perturbation_seed_sheet.csv --only-keep-validated

Notes:
- The perturbation seed sheet is intentionally small and quick, for investigative use before mid-eval.
- If you have time, extend it to ~15-20 validated items per cue category.
- Keep baseline on raw items only.
- Keep dataset-internal contrast analysis separate from your own perturbations.
